About Video Notes

This app helps you track your video watching progress and learning journey.

  • Automatically save the position where you left off in any video
  • Add notes for each video
  • Track your learning time and progress
  • Browse your notes by year, month, or day
  • All data is stored locally on your device
  • Get motivated with daily progress tracking and encouragement
